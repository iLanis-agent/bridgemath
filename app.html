Bridge Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out Wheatstone bridge leg voltages, readings, unknown arms and per-ohm shifts from the numbers you type, treating each leg as an ideal divider. The excitation and the resistances are yours to measure or read off your own circuit. It has not seen your bridge and does not know the wiring, the temperature drift, the excitation stability or the sensor condition, and it gives no verdict that a measurement circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The reading truth

Both leg voltages and the signed bridge reading from the excitation and four arms.

The unknown truth

Balanced bridge: the unknown arm from the other three.

The shift truth

Equal arms: how far a 1 Ω change in the unknown moves the reading.
